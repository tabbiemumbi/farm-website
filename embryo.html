EMBRYO

An embryo is an early stage of development in multicellular organisms, forming immediately after a single-celled zygote (fertilized egg) begins rapid cell division. The term applies to both plant seeds and animal offspring before fully forming mature organs.

Stages of Development

  • Plant Embryogenesis: Fertilized ovule, Zygote, Globular stage, Heart-shaped stage (cotyledon differentiation) , Mature dormant seed embryo.
