CALF

A calf (plural: calves) is a young domestic bovine from birth up to weaning or reaching one year of age. Proper care during early life determines their long-term growth, health, and productivity.

Developmental Stages & Feeding Requirements

  • Colostrum Stage (Day 1-3): Newborns must receive colostrum
  • Pre-Weaning Stage (Week 1-8): Calves rely primarily on milk or milk replacer alongside clean water and calf starter pellets
  • Weaning Stage (Month 2-3): Weaning occurs when the calf consistently consumes sufficient solid starter feed
  • Post-Weaning & Growing: Young stock are raised on high-quality forage and balanced minerals to support
