    • How Honeybees Produce Wax
  • High Energy Input: Bees must consume roughly 6 to 8 pounds of honey to produce just 1 pound of wax.
  • Gland Secretion: Eight specialized wax glands on the underside of the worker bee's abdomen convert the sugar from honey into liquid wax.
  • Hardening: As the liquid wax seeps out of the glands and hits the air, it solidifies into small, clear, flake-like scales.
  • Molding: The bee uses its legs to transfer the wax flakes to its mandibles (jaws)
