CUCUMBER(Cucumis sativus)

Cucumbers (Cucumis sativus) are widely cultivated creeping vine plants in the gourd family (Cucurbitaceae), botanically classified asberries (pepos) though treated as culinary vegetables.

Common Types

  • Slicing: Smooth-skinned, dark green, eaten fresh
  • Pickling: Bumpy-skinned, shorter, used for brine or vinegar preservation (e.g., Kirby).
