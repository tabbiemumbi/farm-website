BANANAS

Bananas are edible fruits produced by large herbaceous flowering plants in the genus Musa. Botanically classified as berries, they grow hanging in large clusters called bunches, which are made up of smaller sub-clusters known as "hands."

Plant Structure & Biology

Despite often being referred to as "banana trees," the plant is actually a giant herb. It lacks a true wooden trunk; instead, its stem consists of tightly packed leaf sheaths called a pseudostem.

Varieties & Culinary Uses:

  • Sweet/Dessert Bananas (e.g., Cavendish): Eaten fresh or used in baking and smoothies once ripe and yellow.
  • Cooking Bananas & Plantains: Starchier varieties that are typically boiled, steamed, fried, or roasted while still green or firm.

    • Nutritional Value: Rich in carbohydrates (primarily sugars in ripe bananas and starch in green ones), dietary fiber, potassium, vitamin B6, and vitamin C.
