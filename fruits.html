FRUITS

Botanically, a fruit is the seed-bearing structure formed from the ovary of a flowering plant after blooming. Below is an overview of common fruit categories, key examples, and their botanical characteristics.

Botanical Categories

  • Pomes: Firm fruits with a central core containing seeds, surrounded by thick edible flesh
  • Drupes (Stone Fruits): Soft flesh surrounding a hard central pit or shell containing a single seed
  • berries: Entirely fleshy fruits derived from a single ovary, usually containing multiple seeds
  • Citrus (Hesperidium): Leathery rind containing aromatic oils, filled with segmented, juicy pulp vesicles
  • Aggregates & Multiples:fruits formed from multiple ovaries within a single flower
