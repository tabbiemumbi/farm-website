SHEEPS

Sheep (Ovis aries) are quadrupedal, ruminant mammals typically kept as livestock for their wool, meat (lamb and mutton), and milk.

Ecological Benefits

  • Targeted Weed Control & Mowing
  • Soil Fertilization & Aeration
  • Fire Fuel Reduction (Conservation Grazing)
  • Multi-Product Yield per Acre

    • Physical Characteristics & Biology

  • Fleece & Coat: Most domestic breeds grow a thick, crimped wool coat that requires annual shearing
  • Horns & Anatomy: Depending on the breed, males (rams) often possess large, spiral horns
  • Ruminant Digestion: They have a four-chambered stomach
  • Vision & Senses: Horizontal, slit-shaped pupils give them wide peripheral vision

    • Social Behavior & Farming

  • Flocking Instinct
  • Herblike Foraging
