BIRDS

Birds are warm-blooded, egg-laying vertebrates characterized primarily by their feathers and forelimbs modified as wings. With over 10,000 living species, they are found in almost every ecosystem on Earth, ranging from the tiny 2-inch bee hummingbird to the towering 9-foot ostrich.
