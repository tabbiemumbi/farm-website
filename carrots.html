CARROTS

The carrot (Daucus carota subsp. sativus) is a versatile, root vegetables belonging to the parsley family (Apiaceae). Highly regarded for its high beta-carotene and vitamin A content, it is cultivated worldwide for its edible taproot and feathery green foliage.
