GOATS

Goats (Capra hircus) are versatile, hardy mammals belonging to the family Bovidae. Domesticated over 10,000 years ago, they are renowned for their agility, intelligence, and adaptable diets.

Digestive System & Feeding

  • ruminant: Have a four-chambered stomach
  • Browsers (Not Grazers): Unlike sheeps or cows that eat grass, goats prefer browsing on leaves

    • Behavior & Intelligence

  • Curious & Inquisitive: Highly curious animals that explore their environment using their sensitive upper lips and tongue.
  • Social Herd Dynamics: Form strong social hierarchies within herds and communicate using distinct vocal bleats and body posture.

    • Nutritional & Food Security

  • Easily Digestible Milk
  • Global Meat Source
  • Consistent Yield
    • Economic & Household Wealth
  • Low Initial Investment: Goats cost significantly less to purchase, feed
  • Financial Reserve & Liquidity: In many developing economies, goats act as a "living bank account.
  • High Reproductive Rate: Goats often give birth to twins or triplets
