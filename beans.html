BEANS

A bean is the edible seed or pod of flowering plants in the family Fabaceae (commonly known as the legume family).

types of beans

  • Pinto Beans
  • Kidney Beans
  • Great Northern Beans

  • Green (Snap) Beans

    • Animal Feed & Livestock

  • Soybean Meal:The solid byproduct remaining after soy oil extraction is the global standard for high-protein poultry
  • Forage & Hay:Whole bean plants (such as fava or velvet beans) are grown as grazing pasture or harvested as hay and silage for cattle.
    • Industrial & Commercial Applications
  • Bioplastics & Biofuels: Soy oil is converted into biodiesel, while bean starches and proteins are used to manufacture biodegradable plastics
  • Inks & Paints: Soy-based inks are widely used in commercial printing as an eco-friendly alternative to petroleum inks.
  • Textiles & Cosmetics:Extracts from soybeans and castor beans supply fatty acids for soaps, lotions
