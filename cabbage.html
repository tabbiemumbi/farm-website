CABBAGES

Cabbages are leafy green, red, or white biennial plants grown as annualvegetable crops they thrive in cool climates and are a staple ingredient in kitchens worldwide.

types of cabbages

  • Green Cabbage
  • Red (Purple) Cabbage
  • White (Dutch White) Cabbage
  • Pointed (Conehead) Cabbage

    • Soil Type

  • Loam to Clay Loam: Cabbages prefer fertile, moisture-retentive soils.
  • Good Drainage: Although they love moisture, they do not tolerate waterlogging
