Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are organic biomolecules composed of carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen atoms (typically following the formula $\text{C}_n(\text{H}_2\text{O})_n$). They serve as the primary source of metabolic energy for living organisms and provide structural support in plants, fungi, and animals.

Structural Classifications

  • Monosaccharides
  • Disaccharides
  • Oligosaccharides
  • Polysaccharides

    • Structural Support

  • Cellulose: Forms rigid plant cell walls (indigestible dietary fiber for non-ruminant animals).
  • Chitin: Forms fungal cell walls and the exoskeletons of insects and crustaceans.
