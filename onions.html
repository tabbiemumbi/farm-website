ONIONS(Allium cepa)

The onion (Allium cepa) is a fundamental bulb vegetables belonging to the amaryllis family (Amaryllidaceae). Essential across nearly all global cuisines, it is cultivated for its layered underground bulb and aromatic, sulfurous compounds.
