SEEDS

A seed is a fertilized, matured ovule of a flowering plant (angiosperm) or gymnosperm that contains an embryonic plant and nutrient reserves enclosed in a protective outer coat. It serves as the primary unit of reproduction, dispersal, and survival for seed-bearing plants.

Anatomy of a Seed

  • Seed Coat (Testa): The hard, protective outer covering that safeguards the embryo
  • Embryo: The miniature young plant consisting of the radicle (developing root), plumule (developing shoot),
  • Cotyledon(s): Embryonic leaves that store nutrients or absorb food stored in the endosperm
  • Endosperm: Starch and protein-rich tissue found in many seeds (like cereals) that feeds the embryo
