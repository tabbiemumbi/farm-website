PEAS(Pisum sativum)

Peas (Pisum sativum) are small, sphericalseeds produced inside long grts belonging to the legume family (Fabaceae).

Physical Characteristics

  • Seeds: Small, round, bright green spheres
  • Pods: Encasing pods grow 2-4 inches long, holding 5-10 seeds each
  • Plant: Climbing vines with tendrils that wrap around supports, bearing white or purple flowers

    • Nutritional Profile

  • Vitamins: Rich in Vitamin C, Vitamin K, Vitamin A, and folate (B9)
  • Minerals: High in iron, manganese, magnesium, and phosphorus.

    • Key Soil Conditions for Peas

  • Soil Texture: Sandy loam is ideal because it warms up quickly in early spring and drains easily.
  • Soil pH: Slightly acidic to neutral, ideally between 6.0 and 7.5.
  • Drainage: Excellent drainage is critical
  • Nutrients: Peas fix their own nitrogen from the air via soil bacteria, so avoid high-nitrogen fertilizers

    • Culinary Uses

  • Soups & Purees
  • Flours & Starches
  • Whole Vegetable Dishes
    • Agriculture & Gardening
  • Cover Crops & Green Manure
  • Forage & Animal Feed
