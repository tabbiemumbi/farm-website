MULCHING

Mulching is the practice of applying a protective layer of material over the surface of the soil around plants. It serves to conserve soil moisture, suppress weed growth, moderate soil temperature, and protect against erosion.

Benefits

  • Moisture Retention
  • Weed Control
  • Temperature Regulation
  • Erosion & Compaction Shield
  • Soil Health Improvement
