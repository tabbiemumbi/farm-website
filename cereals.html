Cereals

Cereals are cultivated grasses belonging to the Poaceae family grown primarily for their edible starchy seeds (caryopses or grains). They serve as the primary staple food worldwide, supplying the majority of dietary energy and carbohydrates for human consumption.

Anatomy of a Cereal Grain

  • Bran: The hard outer shell rich in dietary fiber, B vitamins, and trace minerals.
  • Endosperm: The large middle layer containing complex carbohydrates
  • Germ: The nutrient-denseembryo packed with healthy fats, vitamin E
