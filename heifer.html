HEIFERS

A heifer is a young female bovine (cattle) that has not yet given birth to a calf. Once she gives birth to her first calf, she officially transitions from being called a heifer to a cow.

Management & Development Stages

  • Breeding Age: Heifers typically reach puberty around 9-12 months, but artificial insemination
  • Gestation: A heifer's pregnancy lasts about 9 months
  • Calving & Milk Production: After her first calving (around 22-24 months of age), her mammary glands fully develop
