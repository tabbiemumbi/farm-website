ARROWROOTS(Maranta arundinacea)

Arrowroot (Maranta arundinacea) is a tropical tuber native to Rainforest environments in Central and South America. Widely cultivated across tropical regions, it is prized both as a whole, starchy root crop and as a refined, grain-free starch powder.

Culinary Starch & Powder Uses

  • Dairy-Free Ice Creams
  • Crispy Coating
  • Gluten-Free & Paleo Baking

    • Whole Fresh Tuber Uses

  • Breakfast & Energy Staple
  • Stews & Curries
  • Roasted or Fried

    • Health, Cosmetic & Other Application

  • Skincare Thickener
  • Dry Shampoo & Body Powder
  • Digestive Soothing
