TOMATOES(Solanum lycopersicum)

The tomato (Solanum lycopersicum) is an edible berriesberry originating from western South America and belongs to the nightshade family (Solanaceae). Though botanically classified as a fruits,it is legally and culinarily treated as a vegetable.

Nutritional Highlights

  • High in Vitamin C, Vitamin K, Potassium, and Folate.
  • Rich source of lycopene

    • Culinary Uses

  • Raw Applications
  • Cooked Bases
  • Soups & Broths
  • Preserved & Processed
  • Beverages

    • Dietary & Health Benefits

  • Antioxidant Source: High in lycopene, which supports heart health and protects cells against oxidative damage.
  • Nutrient Boost: Provides essential vitamins (C, K, A) and potassium for immune support
  • Hydration: Composed of roughly 95% water, making them an excellent hydrating ingredient.
    • Non-Culinary Applications
  • Skincare: Tomato extract and pulp are used in home remedies and cosmetics for gentle exfoliation and brightening due to natural skin-friendly acids.
  • Composting & Gardening: Overripe or decaying tomatoes can be added to compost
