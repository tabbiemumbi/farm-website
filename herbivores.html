HERBIVORES

Herbivores are animals biologically adapted to consume plant material-such as leaves, grass,seeds, fruit, and wood-as the primary component of their diet. Because plant cell walls contain tough cellulose that is difficult to break down, herbivores have evolved specialized anatomical and digestive adaptations.

Key Anatomical Adaptations

  • Teeth Structure: Broad, flat molars and premolars for grinding fibrous plant tissue, along with sharp incisors for clipping stems
  • Eyes Placement: Located on the sides of the head to provide a wide, panoramic field of view to detect incoming predators.
  • Digestive Systems: Extended digestive tracts or multi-chambered stomachs containing specialized microflora
