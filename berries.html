BERRIES

Botanically, a true berry is a fleshy fruit produced from a single ovary without a hard pit (stony endocarp) inside. In common language, however, the term refers to any small, pulpy, edible fruit.

Botanical vs. Common Classification

  • True Botanical Berries: Fleshy fruits derived from one ovary containing embedded seeds.
  • Aggregate Fruits (Botanically "False Berries"): Derived from a single flower with multiple separate pistils
  • Accessory Fruits: The edible pulpy part develops from the flower receptacle rather than the ovary itself
