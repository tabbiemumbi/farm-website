SMOOTHIES

A smoothie is a thick, creamy beverage made by pureeing whole raw ingredients-typically fruits, vegetables, and a liquid base-in a high-speed blender. Unlike strained juices, smoothies retain the natural dietary fiber of their solid ingredients, giving them a rich, heavy texture.

Components

  • Produce Base: Fresh or frozen fruits (like bananas, berries, or mangoes) and leafy greens or vegetables (such as spinach, kales, or carrots).
  • Liquid Base: Provides the necessary fluid for blending
  • Thickeners & Texture Boosters: Ingredients like yogurt, avocados, peanut butter, chia seeds, or oats help build a smooth, velvety body.
  • Nutritional Add-ins: Protein powders, flaxseeds, spirulina, honey, or spices like cinnamon and ginger are often added for extra flavor and health benefits.
