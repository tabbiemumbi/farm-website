CATTLES

Cattle (Bos taurus and Bos indicus) are large, domesticated, cloven-hoofed herbivores raised globally for agriculture, draft power, and ecological management.

Terminology & Classifications

  • Cow: Adult female that has given birth to at least one calf.
  • Bull: Intact adult male used primarily for breeding.
  • Steer: Castrated male raised for beef production.
  • Heifer: Young female that has not yet borne a calf.
