SPINACH(Spinacia oleracea)

Spinach (Spinacia oleracea) is an edible flowering plant in the family Amaranthaceae, native to central and western Asia. Cultivated globally for its nutrient-rich green leaves, it is a staple leafy vegetable eaten both raw and cooked.

Culinary Preparation

  • Cooked: Heat collapses spinach volume significantly. Steaming
  • Raw: Baby spinach leaves feature a mild, slightly sweet flavor ideal for salads, sandwiches, and green juices.
