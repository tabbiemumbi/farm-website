ANIMALS Feed

Animal feeds are food materials formulated specifically for livestock, poultry, and domestic animals to supply essential nutrients-proteins, carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, and minerals-needed for growth, milk production, egg laying, work, and general health maintenance.

Categories of Animal Feed

  • Forage
  • Energy Concentrates
  • Protein Supplements
  • Compound Feed (Pellets/Mash)
  • Mineral & Vitamin Supplements
