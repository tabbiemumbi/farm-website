RUMINANT MAMMALS

Ruminants are herbivorous mammals that digest plant-based food through specialized multi-chambered stomachs and microbial fermentation. They undergo rumination-regurgitating partially digested feed (cud), re-chewing it, and swallowing it again to break down fibrous plant materials like cellulose.

Common Examples & Classification

  • True Ruminants: Cattle,sheep, goats, deer, giraffes, antelopes, and buffalo
  • Pseudoruminants: Camels, llamas, and alpacas-they chew cud
