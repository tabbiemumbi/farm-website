HORSES

Horses (Equus caballus) are large, single-toed odd-toed ungulates that evolved over 50 million years from small multi-toed creatures into swift, powerfulherbivoresadapted for open grasslands.

Major Breed Categories

  • Hotbloods: Known for speed, stamina, and high energy
  • Coldbloods: Heavy, muscular draft horses built for slow, powerful work like hauling and plowing.
  • Warmbloods: Crosses between hot and cold breeds, combining athleticism with calm temperaments.
  • Ponies: Defined strictly as horses standing under 14.2 hands (58 inches) at the shoulder,
