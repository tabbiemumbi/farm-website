RABBITS

Rabbits are small, herbivores mammals belonging to the family Leporidae in the order Lagomorpha. Found in wild environments across the globe and kept widely as domestic pets,

Body Language & Posture

  • Flattening / Freezing: Pressing their body flat against the ground with ears pinned tightly back.
  • Ear Positions
  • Perching / Standing up: Rising on their hind legs to get a higher vantage point
  • Nose Nudging: Tapping their nose against your hand or foot
  • The Bunny Flop: Suddenly rolling onto their side and lying still with legs stretched out

    • Physical Actions & Behaviors

  • Foot Thumping
  • Chin Grooming ("Chin Line")
  • Zoomies
  • Licking / Grooming

    • Appetite & Digestive Indicators

  • Selective Eating
  • Changes in Fecal Pellets
  • Excess Cecotropes
    • Physical Signs of a stressed rabbit
  • Wet Fur around Mouth or Chin
  • Heavy or Rapid Breathing
