MANGOES

Mangoes (Mangifera indica) are tropical stone fruits belonging to the Anacardiaceae (cashew) family, native to South Asia. Known as the "king of fruits," they are cultivated worldwide in tropical and subtropical climates for their sweet, aromatic flesh.

Nutritional Profile & Health Benefits

  • Vitamin C & A: A single serving provides over 60% of daily Vitamin C requirements and high levels of beta-carotene for immune and eye health.
  • Dietary Fiber & Enzymes: Contains digestive enzymes like amylases and soluble fiber, aiding gut health and digestion.
  • Antioxidants: Rich in polyphenols, including mangiferin, which help combat oxidative stress.

    • Common Uses

  • Fresh Consumption: Eaten raw when ripe or sliced into salads, smoothies, and desserts.
  • Culinary Applications: Green (unripe) mangoes are used in pickles, chutneys, and savory dishes
