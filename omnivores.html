OMNIVORES

Omnivores are animals biologically adapted to consume and derive energy from both plant material and animal matter. This dietary flexibility allows them to thrive in diverse environments and adapt easily to changing food availability.

Ecological Significance

  • Trophic Flexibility: Can act as both primary consumers (eating producers) and secondary/tertiary consumers
  • Ecosystem Resilience: Less vulnerable to population crashes caused by the collapse of a single food source.
  • Environmental Impact: Often serve as key seedsdispersers and scavengers that assist in nutrient recycling.
