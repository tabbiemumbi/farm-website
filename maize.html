MAIZE(Zea mays)

Maize (Zea mays), commonly referred to as corn in North America, is a tall annual cereal grass belonging to the Poaceae family. it is now one of the world's most vital staple food crops alongsidewheat and rice.

    • Industrial & Commercial Applications

  • Biofuels: Fermented to produce fuel-grade ethanol
  • Bioplastics & Packaging: Polylactic acid (PLA) derived from corn starch is used to produce biodegradable cups
  • Paper & Textiles: Starch acts as a sizing agent to strengthen paper fibers and coat fabrics during manufacturing.

    • Human Food & Food Processing

  • Direct Food: Prepared boiled or roasted as whole ears (sweet corn) or popped as a snack (popcorn).
  • Staple Foods: Ground into cornmeal, masa, or flour to produce tortillas, polenta, ugali, porridge, and cornbread.
