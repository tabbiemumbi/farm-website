ANIMALS

Animals are multicellular, eukaryotic organisms belonging to the kingdom Animalia. Unlike plants and fungi, animals lack rigid cell walls, ingest organic material for energy, and typically possess specialized tissue systems (nervous, muscular, and digestive) that enable movement and rapid response to their environment.

Dietary Guilds

  • herbivores: Animals biologically adapted to consume plant matter (e.g., cattle, rabbits, elephants).
  • Carnivores: Specialized predators or scavengers that feed on meat and animal tissues (e.g., lions, eagles, sharks).
  • Omnivores:Flexible feeders that consume both plant and animal matter (e.g., humans, pigs, bears).
