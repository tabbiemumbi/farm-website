KALES(Brassica oleracea var. acephala)

Kale (Brassica oleracea var.acephala) aent-dense leafy green vegetable belonging to the cabbage (Brassicaceae) family. Unlike headcabbages, its central leaves do not form a tight head, growing instead as loose, upright fronds around a central stalk.

    • Flavor Profile

  • Raw: Earthy, peppery, slightly bitter, and robust.
  • Cooked: Milder, sweeter, and more tender

    • Common Varieties

  • Curly Kale: Dark green, heavily ruffled leaves with a strong, peppery flavor
  • Lacinato (Tuscan / Dinosaur) Kale: Long, flat, dark blue-green leaves with a bumpy, reptile-skin texture; sweeter and tenderer than curly kale.
  • Redbor Kale: Vibrant deep red to purple leaves with frilly edges
  • Baby Kale: Young kale leaves harvested early; soft, mild, and eaten raw likespinach.
