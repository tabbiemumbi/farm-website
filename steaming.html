STEAMING

Steaming is a moist-heat cooking technique where food is cooked using the vaporized heat of boiling water. The food is placed above the water in a perforated container or basket so that hot steam surrounds it without submerging the ingredients directly in liquid.

Key Advantages & Best Uses

  • Healthier Preparation: Requires no added oils, butter, or fats to prevent sticking or facilitate heat transfer.
  • Texture & Appearance: Preserves the natural color, crispness, and structural integrity of foods without making them mushy or dried out.
  • Ideal Foods: Green vegetables (broccoli, spinach, peas), root tubers (potatoes, carrots,cassava), delicate seafood, dumplings, tamales, and rice.
