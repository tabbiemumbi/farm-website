PESTS

Pests are organisms-including insects, mites, nematodes, rodents, weeds, and fungi-that cause damage to agricultural crops, livestock, stored food, or human environments. In agriculture, pests reduce crop yields, transmit viral or bacterial plant diseases, and degrade post-harvest quality.
